An entry for the [make.fun bounty "One Star, Five Stars for the Food"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-one-star-five-stars-for-the-food-f9166ee7): one sentence, at most 200 characters, in the voice of an alien tourist giving planet Earth a one-star review while still slipping in a real compliment about the food.

The review: *"One star: Earth makes me breathe its air for free then bills me for water, but the ramen broth alone justifies the invasion."*

Why this line: the complaint is genuinely alien — an outsider baffled that air is free but water comes with a bill — and the food compliment is the punchline itself, slipped in as the reason the invasion was worth it.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the review to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set).
